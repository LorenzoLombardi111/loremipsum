Step-by-Step: Adding Lorem Ipsum to Figma

Matching Text Length to Figma Auto-Layout Frames

Auto-layout is central to modern Figma workflows, and the length of your placeholder text directly affects how frames resize. Too little text and your components look artificially compact; too much and they overflow or push sibling elements out of alignment. With freeloremipsum.app you can dial in the exact word count before pasting, check the counter, and know that the text will behave predictably inside hug-content and fill-container frames.
